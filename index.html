Welcome to Kenya Marine Supply Ltd

We are a leading provider of marine products and services in Kenya, dedicated to delivering exceptional quality and customer service.

core business activities

Kenya Marine Supply Ltd offers a wide range of marine products and services to meet the diverse needs of our clients. Our core business activities include:

  • Supply of marine equipment and components
  • Marine maintenance and repair services
  • Coastal marine transport solutions
  • Harbor service management

History

Kenya Marine Supply Ltd is a leading supplier of marine products and services in Kenya. Established in 1995, the company has built a strong reputation for providing high-quality marine equipment and solutions to clients across the country. With a dedicated team of professionals, Kenya Marine Supply Ltd is committed to delivering exceptional service and support to its customers.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide superior marine products and services that meet the needs of our customers while maintaining the highest standards of quality and safety. We are committed to providing innovative solutions and exceptional customer service.

Our vision is to be the premier leader in the East African maritime sector by delivering innovative, safe, and sustainable marine solutions that drive the regional blue economy forward.
